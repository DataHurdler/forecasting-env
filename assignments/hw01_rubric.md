# Homework 01 Rubric (100 points)

## 1) Use Case Quality and Relevance (30 pts)
- 27-30: Three or more strong and distinct real-world use cases, clearly relevant.
- 20-26: Use cases are valid but somewhat repetitive or underdeveloped.
- 0-19: Missing, weak, or unclear use cases.

## 2) Tools and Techniques Explanation (30 pts)
- 27-30: Clear explanation of tools and methods for each case; technically accurate.
- 20-26: Mostly accurate with minor gaps in depth or clarity.
- 0-19: Major inaccuracies or missing method/tool explanations.

## 3) Cross-Case Comparison and Insight (20 pts)
- 18-20: Strong synthesis; explains tradeoffs and context-fit decisions.
- 13-17: Basic comparison with limited insight.
- 0-12: Minimal comparison or unsupported conclusions.

## 4) Evidence and Sources (10 pts)
- 9-10: At least 3 credible citations, clearly tied to claims.
- 6-8: Sources present but weakly integrated.
- 0-5: Insufficient or low-quality sourcing.

## 5) Policy and Submission Compliance (10 pts)
- 10: Fully compliant with required files, prompt log, prompt limit, and commit-per-prompt policy.
- 5-9: Minor compliance issues.
- 0-4: Major missing artifacts or policy violations.
